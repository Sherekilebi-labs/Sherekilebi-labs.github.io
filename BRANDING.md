# Brand and media notes

The visual direction interprets the lab's own eccentric-builder identity through original writing, a warm workshop palette, and a flight-log layout. It does not reproduce a film poster, scene composition, dialogue, soundtrack, or film footage. The supplied logo and Tsatmfreni artwork are used on the basis of the owner's explicit confirmation that they are original or permitted artwork.

The name's inspiration is acknowledged in normal descriptive text: Eldar Shengelaia's *The Eccentrics* / *Sherekilebi*. The lab does not claim affiliation, endorsement, or ownership of the film. Film material remains with its rights holders. Attribution alone does not grant a right to reuse that material.

Georgia's [Law on Copyright and Related Rights](https://matsne.gov.ge/en/document/view/16198) distinguishes ideas and concepts from protected expression and treats audiovisual works as protected works. The design therefore takes a broad idea—improbable invention—and expresses it using the lab's own writing, artwork, and project photographs. This is a design boundary, not a legal determination that every possible use of a film-related name or image is cleared.

The [National Archives of Georgia](https://archive.gov.ge/en/news/erovnuli-arkivis-kinoteatrshi-eldar-shengelaias-filmebis-retrospektiva-1) identifies *Eccentrics* among Shengelaia's films. No archive images or film media are copied into the site.

The site now uses system Helvetica, including Helvetica Bold for large titles. It does not bundle or redistribute Helvetica or Eurostile font binaries. JetBrains Mono is the only bundled webfont and includes its SIL OFL notice. No Eurostile Extended or Michroma title font is requested.

Production copies only referenced, reviewed public assets. The supplied photo of watching a program while printing, and other unused source photos, are not included. Future dispatches should continue using lab-owned or permitted photographs and original captions; film clips, soundtrack, posters, and screenshots need their own rights assessment before being added.
